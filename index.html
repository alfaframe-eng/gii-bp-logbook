Before You Begin

Screening and education only. This application does not diagnose hypertension or replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.
Records are stored in this browser on this device. Do not use this application for identifiable patient records on a shared device until appropriate privacy safeguards are established. Browser data can be lost.

Blood pressure is recorded as systolic/diastolic pressure in millimetres of mercury (mmHg). Readings should be taken using a suitable, validated blood pressure monitor and appropriate measurement technique.
